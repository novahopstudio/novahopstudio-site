Bridge Hero — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what data is used by the game Bridge Hero, published by NovaHop Studio, and why.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (best score, coins, heroes, settings, goals, achievements, best runs) is stored only on your phone. We do not collect it and it is not sent to any NovaHop Studio server. Uninstalling the game or using “Delete save” removes it.

2. Google Play Games (global leaderboard)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games gamer name, avatar and best score are sent to Google to display the global leaderboard. This data is handled by Google under its privacy policy. You can manage your profile visibility and sign out in the Google Play Games app or in Android settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect data such as the device advertising ID, IP address, device model and ad interaction information. This data is handled by Google under its policies: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). The “No Ads” purchase removes mandatory ads.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. NovaHop Studio never receives your payment details or address.

5. Children

The game is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Your rights

NovaHop Studio does not hold any personal data about you on its own servers. For data processed by Google (Play Games, AdMob, Google Play), you can exercise your rights of access, correction and deletion with Google, from your Google Account.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page shows the latest change.

8. Contact

Questions: contact@novahopstudio.com
